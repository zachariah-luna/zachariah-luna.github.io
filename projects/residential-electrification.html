Residential electrification + solar/EV analysis

Can an existing gas home switch to a heat pump, induction range, heat-pump water heater and EV charger without a panel upgrade, and how much solar and battery would it take to pay off?

Planned — Dec 2026 · NEC 220.83, PVWatts, Excel/Python

Problem

Many California homes have 100 A or 125 A panels. Going all-electric often triggers a costly panel or service upgrade. I wanted to find out whether a typical home actually needs one, and what solar and storage would change.

Describe the house: size, climate zone, existing panel rating, current gas appliances, utility rate plan.

Design

Method:

  • Existing-dwelling load calculation per NEC 220.83 for each electrification step.
  • Solar production estimate with NREL PVWatts.
  • EV charging and battery sizing based on daily driving and time-of-use rates.
  • Simple payback and cost comparison.
