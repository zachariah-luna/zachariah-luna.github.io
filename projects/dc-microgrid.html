Solar + battery DC microgrid with ESP32 load shedding

A small 12 V solar and LiFePO4 system where an ESP32 monitors the battery and sheds lower-priority loads so a Meshtastic mesh node stays online through cloudy stretches.

Planned — Jan–Mar 2027 · ESP32, LiFePO4, MPPT, Meshtastic

Problem

Off-grid nodes fail when the battery runs flat. A controller that drops non-critical loads early can keep the critical load running much longer. This project builds and measures that idea on a bench-scale microgrid.

State the target: e.g. keep the Meshtastic node running for N days with no sun.

Design

  • Power budget for each load (critical vs. sheddable).
  • Panel, charge controller and battery sizing.
  • ESP32 measures voltage and current and switches loads with MOSFETs or relays at set thresholds.
  • Fusing, wire sizing and protection.
