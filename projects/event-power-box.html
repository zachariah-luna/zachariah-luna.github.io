Event power distribution box

A portable power distribution box for live events: branch breakers, GFCI protection, metering and a load display, so audio and lighting gear get safe, clean power from shore power or a generator.

Design — Spring 2027 · NEC, load calcs, CAD

Problem

At events, power is often a tangle of extension cords and power strips. Overloaded circuits trip mid-show, and poor grounding adds hum to the sound system. A proper distro box fixes both.

Design

  • One-line diagram and load calculation for a typical event.
  • Breaker and GFCI selection, enclosure, connectors.
  • Metering for voltage, current and load per circuit.
  • Grounding approach to keep audio quiet.
